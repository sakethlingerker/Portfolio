BACKGROUND & IDENTITY

Systems Engineer.
AI Developer. Systems Mindset.

I am Saketh Lingerker, a Systems Engineer and AI Developer focused on building scalable software systems, applied AI solutions, and production-grade applications. My work centers on the intersection of modern software architecture and practical artificial intelligence — ensuring systems are performant, reliable, and grounded in concrete requirements.

During my research internship at the Speech Lab, Language Technologies Research Center (LTRC), IIIT Hyderabad, I researched Voice Activity Detection (VAD) algorithms, developing audio preprocessing pipelines and acoustic feature extractors to advance AI-assisted video summarization in noisy acoustic environments.

Through rigorous technical evaluations, I achieved 89% in the TCS iON Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET-IT) exam and 91.51% in the TCS iON Cognitive NQT, securing an offer as an Incoming Systems Engineer at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS Digital).

Whether architecting an end-to-end MLOps pipeline on AWS EC2/ECR with automated CI/CD runners, designing a high-precision enterprise RAG agent with hybrid vector search, or crafting responsive full-stack web applications, I bring disciplined engineering rigor to every layer of the stack.
